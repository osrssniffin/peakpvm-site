How this works

It compares live prices with recent hourly trade history and looks for the weekday and time block where the original PvM supply basket has tended to be cheapest. Added supplies do not change the overall market indicators. Individual signals require at least 24 traded hourly samples and a quote from the last hour. It is a historical buying signal, not a prediction of future prices.
