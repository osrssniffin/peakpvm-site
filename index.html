How to get in and start sending.

Peak PvM is supposed to be simple. Join the Discord, verify your RSN, grab your activity roles, and start finding content.

1

Join the Discord

Use the official invite and enter the server. That’s your front door into the community.

2

Verify your RSN

Run the RSN onboarding command to unlock the server and get connected to the community setup.

3

Grab roles and PvM

Pick your pings, check the scheduler, and jump into raids, bossing, loot posts, and future events.
