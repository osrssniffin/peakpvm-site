How to get in and start sending.

Peak PvM is supposed to be easy. Join the Discord, verify your RSN, grab the roles you care about, and start finding content.

1

Join the Discord

Hit the official invite, land in the server, and head to the access/onboarding area.

2

Verify your RSN

Use the quick RSN command to unlock the rest of the server and get linked into the community setup.

3

Grab pings and send

Choose the roles for the content you care about, check the scheduler, and jump into raids, bosses, events, and loot posts.

Small community. Actually active.

The goal is simple: more people to raid with, more people to talk OSRS with, and more reasons to log in and play together instead of sitting in a dead server full of lurkers.
